How do I restore a purchase?

On the paywall, tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I export my data?

Use the in-app CSV export. Files are written on your device and shared through the system share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to Lift Focus servers (there are none).

What happens after the free trial?

Lift Focus includes a 10-day free trial. After it ends, a one-time purchase unlocks full access forever. There is no subscription.

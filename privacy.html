Lift Focus · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy describes how Lift Focus (“the app”) handles information. The app is developed by an independent developer. For questions, email liftfocusapp@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Workout plans, workout history, and related settings are stored on your device as local files and preferences. This data is used only to run the app’s features.

A free-trial start date may be stored in the iCloud Keychain (when available on your device) so the trial is not reset by deleting and reinstalling the app. That stamp is not used for advertising or analytics.

What is not collected

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or advertising trackers
  • No crash-reporting SDKs that send data to the developer
  • No Lift Focus servers — the developer does not host your workouts

Export

If you use CSV export, the app writes temporary files on your device and opens the system share sheet. Where those files go next (google sheets, email, Files, another app) is controlled by you. Export does not send data to the developer.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide purchase status needed to unlock the app. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for how Apple handles payment information.

Children

Lift Focus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete the app to remove on-device data. Exported copies you shared elsewhere are under your control. For purchase questions (refunds, billing), contact Apple or use Restore Purchases in the app with your Apple ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
